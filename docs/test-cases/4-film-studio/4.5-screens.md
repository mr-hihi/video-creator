### TC-FS-55: Episode popup — open, close, reload, delete
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** P1 with E1 `draft` and E2 `draft`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Navigate to `/films/{P1}`; in `Episodes`, click the title of `E1`
  2. Press Esc
  3. Click the title of `E1`; click `Close`
  4. Click the title of `E1`; press the browser's Back button
  5. Click the title of `E1`; reload the page
  6. Navigate to `/films/{P1}`; click the title of `E2`; click `Delete`; confirm
  7. `DELETE F/episodes/{E1}`; without reloading `/films/{P1}`, click the title of `E1`
- **Expected:**
  1. A popup opens over the project page, showing `E1 · {title}`, the `Start` button, the stage list, `Outline`
     and `Script`, with `Close` and no link to the project; URL becomes `/films/{P1}/episodes/1`
  2. The popup closes without a question; URL becomes `/films/{P1}`; the project page is still shown
  3. Same as 2
  4. Same as 2
  5. The full episode page is shown, with the link `Test Series` to the project and no popup
  6. Confirm `Delete episode 2? Its scenes and files are removed.`; the popup closes; URL becomes
     `/films/{P1}`; `Episodes` no longer lists `E2`
  7. The popup shows `Episode not found` with `Close`

### TC-FS-56: Episode popup — closing with an unsaved edit
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** P1 with E2 `paused` after `generating_scene_images` (3 scenes).

  1. Navigate to `/films/{P1}`; click the title of `E2`; open `Part 1`
  2. In `Scene 1`, fill `Image prompt` with `A lighthouse at dusk`; click `Close`

  4. Press Esc; in the dialog, click `OK`
  5. Click the title of `E2`; open `Part 1`
  6. Click `Close`
- **Expected:**
  2. The browser dialog `Discard unsaved changes?` appears
  3. The popup stays open; `Image prompt` of `Scene 1` still reads `A lighthouse at dusk` and its `Save` is
     enabled
  4. The same dialog appears; after `OK` the popup closes and URL becomes `/films/{P1}`
  5. `Image prompt` of `Scene 1` shows its saved text, not `A lighthouse at dusk`
  6. The popup closes without a question

### TC-FS-65: Outline panel
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** P1 with E9 `paused` after `writing_outline`: part 1 `The Dive` (`plan` `Mai dives.`, 300 s),
  part 2 `The City` (`plan` `Mai finds the city.`, 300 s), no scene. E2 `paused` after
  `generating_scene_images` with one part of 3 scenes, one with a failed image.
- **Steps:**
  1. Navigate to `/films/{P1}/episodes/{E9 number}`
  2. On `Part 2`, click `Edit`; fill `Length (s)` with `700`; click `Save`
  3. Fill `Length (s)` with `240` and `Plan` with `Mai finds the drowned city.`; click `Save`
  4. On `Part 1`, click `Insert part after`; fill `Title` with `The Storm`, `Plan` with
     `A storm hits the boat.`, `Length (s)` with `120`; click `Save`
  5. On `The Storm`, click `Move up`
  6. On `The Storm`, click `Delete`; click `OK`
  7. Click `Continue`; wait until `Writing script` shows `Part {k} of 2`
  8. Navigate to `/films/{P1}/episodes/2`; open `Part 1`
  9. In the scene with the failed image, click `Generate` under the image; close `Part 1`; wait until the
     request ends
- **Expected:**
  1. `Outline` lists two closed blocks: `Part 1 · The Dive` · `300 s` · `Not written` and `Part 2 · The City` ·
     `300 s` · `Not written`; each shows `Write part`, no `Rewrite part`; counters read `Parts 2 · Scenes 0 · …`
  2. The alert shows `Part length must be between 8 and 600 seconds`; the form keeps `700`
  3. `Part 2 · The City` · `240 s`; the plan reads `Mai finds the drowned city.`
  4. A new `Part 2 · The Storm` · `120 s`; `The City` becomes `Part 3`
  5. `The Storm` becomes `Part 1`
  6. Dialog `Delete part 1?`; two parts remain, numbered 1–2
  7. While the episode runs, `Edit`, `Delete`, `Write part` and the move buttons are disabled with the hint
     `Stop the episode to edit.`
  8. `Part 1` header shows `3 scenes`, `Images 2/3 · Clips 0/3` and badge `1 failed`; the three scene cards
     appear under it; `Rewrite part` is not shown — its scenes have images
  9. Without a reload, the closed `Part 1` header turns to `Images 3/3 · Clips 0/3` with no `failed` badge,
     and the counters line to `Images 3/3`

### TC-FS-66: Story bible tab
- **App:** Frontend + API
- **Precondition:** P1 with `storyBible` written by E1 (`storyBibleEpisodeId` = E1).
- **Steps:**
  1. Navigate to `/films/{P1}?tab=story-bible`
  2. Append ` Mai has a sister.` to the text; click `Save`
  3. Fill the text area with 20001 characters
  4. Undo to the saved text; type ` Draft`; click the tab `Overview`; click `Cancel` in the dialog
  5. *(API unit)* `PATCH F/projects/{P1}` `{"storyBible":"x"}` while an episode of P1 runs `updating_story_bible`
- **Expected:**
  1. The text area holds the story bible with the counter `{n} / 20000`; the line reads `Updated by E1 · {time}`;
     `Save` is disabled
  2. Notice `Saved`; the line reads `Edited by you · {time}`; `GET F/projects/{P1}` → `storyBibleEpisodeId` =
     `null`
  3. The counter reads `20001 / 20000`; `Save` shows the alert
     `Story bible must be at most 20000 characters` and keeps the text
  4. Dialog `Discard unsaved changes?`; after `Cancel` the tab `Story bible` stays with ` Draft` in the text
  5. 409 `Episode {number} is updating the story bible — try again when it finishes`; the story bible is
     unchanged
