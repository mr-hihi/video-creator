### TC-FS-41: Header and Film Studio links
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** P1 with episode 1.
- **Steps:**
  1. Navigate to http://localhost:5071/films
  2. Click `Library`
  3. Click `Projects`; click `Test Series`
  4. Click episode `E1`
- **Expected:**
  - Every page shows the header items `Video to Markdown`, `Film Analysis`, `Film Clone`, `Film Studio`,
    `Settings` in that order, with `Film Studio` highlighted, and the links `Projects` · `Library` under it
  - Step 2: URL becomes `/films/library`, the `Characters` tab is open
  - Step 3: URL becomes `/films/{P1}`
  - Step 4: URL becomes `/films/{P1}/episodes/1`

### TC-FS-43: Project page
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** P1 (style `realistic`) with an uploaded scene image in one episode; linked C1 (reference
  `done`), C2 (`none`) and C5 `Neon Cat` (`creature`, style `anime`, reference `failed` with
  `Blocked by the content filter`).
- **Steps:**
  1. Navigate to `/films/{P1}`
  2. In `Settings`, select `6 s` from `Default clip length`; click `Save`
  3. Click `Archive`
  4. Click `Unarchive`
  5. Click `New episode`; click `Edit presets`
- **Expected:**
  - `Cast`: C1 shows its image and `Regenerate`, `Edit image`, `Upload`, `Remove from project`; C2 shows
    `Generate`; C5 shows `Failed: Blocked by the content filter`, `Generate` and badge `Different style`;
    the button reads `Generate missing references (2)`
  - `Frame` and `Style` are disabled with the hint `Fixed once a scene image exists`; `Save` is disabled before
    the change
  - Step 2: notice `Saved`
  - Step 3: badge `Archived`, notice `This project is archived.`; every form and button is disabled except
    `Unarchive` and `Delete`
  - Step 4: the forms are enabled again
  - Step 5: the `New episode` panel has the default preset preselected as `{name} (default)`; URL becomes
    `/settings?tab=prompt-override&id={default preset id}`

### TC-FS-44: Episode page by status
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** In P1: E1 `draft`; E2 `paused` after `generating_scene_images` (3 scenes, 3 images `done`,
  narration off); E3 `failed` at `generating_clips` with `1 clip(s) failed — fix them and resume`; E4 `done`
  with `needsAssembly` `true`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Open each of `/films/{P1}/episodes/1` … `/4`
- **Expected:**
  - E1: button `Start`; every stage `upcoming`
  - E2: button `Continue`; `Generating scene images` is `paused`; the page reads
    `Paused after Generating scene images — check the work, then Continue.`; counters
    `Scenes 3 · Images 3/3 · Clips 0/3 · Narration 0/0`; no narration part on the cards
  - E3: button `Resume`; `Generating clips` is `failed`; error alert `1 clip(s) failed — fix them and resume`;
    the failed scene shows its clip error
  - E4: button `Assemble again` with the notice `Scenes changed since the last assembly.`; `Result` shows the
    player, `Download video` and `Generate thumbnails`
  - E1–E4 show `Delete`; `Rewrite script` only on E1 and E2 — E2 only if it had no image, so here only E1

### TC-FS-52: Cancel buttons
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** No Google Flow extension connected; `filmStudioImageProvider` setting `google_flow`. A project P, a linked
  character C whose reference is `none`, and episode 1 of P — `draft` or `paused` — whose scene 1 has no image.
- **Steps:**
  1. Navigate to `/films/{P}`; on the C card click `Generate`
  2. Click `Cancel` on the C card
  3. Navigate to `/films/{P}/episodes/1`; in `Scene 1` click `Generate` under the image
  4. Click `Cancel` under the image of `Scene 1`
- **Expected:**
  1. The card shows status `Queued` and a `Cancel` button
  2. `Cancel` disappears; the card's status is cleared and `Generate` is enabled again
  3. The image part shows status `Queued` and a `Cancel` button; the other actions of the scene are disabled
  4. `Cancel` disappears; the image part shows no status and `Generate` is enabled again
