## Creation

### TC-FS-11: Create an episode from an idea and pause after the script
- **App:** Frontend
- **Precondition:** P1 with C1 and C2 linked; the default preset at its current version.
- **Steps:**
  1. Navigate to `/films/{P1}`; click `New episode`
  2. Keep source `Idea`; fill `Idea` with `Mai finds a glowing compass in the harbor fog and follows it to a sunken bell.`
  3. Fill `Target length` with `16`
  4. In `Pause after`, tick `Script` and keep `Scene images` ticked
  5. *(captcha on)* Wait for the captcha; click `Create and start`
  6. Wait until the status badge shows `paused`
- **Expected:**
  - Step 3: the hint reads `The outline splits it into parts of up to 10 minutes; the script picks each scene's length, 4-30 s.`
  - The episode popup opens over the project page; URL becomes `/films/{P1}/episodes/1`; `Writing outline` is
    `completed`, `Writing script` is `paused` and the popup reads
    `Paused after Writing script — check the work, then Continue.`; a `Continue` button is shown;
    `Updating story bible` is `upcoming`
  - `Outline` shows at least one part block, each with a title, `{target} s` and `{n} scenes`; opening
    `Part 1` shows at least one scene card, each with a non-empty `Image prompt` and `Video prompt`, a
    `Clip length (s)` of 4–30, every image, clip and narration status `none`
  - `Script` shows a non-empty logline, `Music prompt` and `Thumbnail prompt`, an empty `Summary`,
    `Prompt preset` = `{default preset name} v{n}`, `Source` = `Idea`
  - `GET F/episodes/{id}` → `status` = `paused`, `step` = `writing_script`, `number` = 1, `targetDurationS` = 16,
    `summary` = `null`; `filmOutlinePrompt`, `filmScriptPrompt` and `storyBiblePrompt` = the default preset's
    current `filmOutline`, `filmScript` and `storyBible`; every part has `sceneCount` ≥ 1
  - `GET F/parts/{part 1 id}` → every scene has `trimStartMs` = 1000
  - Every character a scene lists is in P1's cast; P1's `storyBible` is still `null`
