# 4.4 Audio & assembly

Part of [4. Film Studio](../4-film-studio.md). Stage `generating_audio` asks providers for narration and
music; stage `assembling` cuts and joins the clips with `ffmpeg`. Thumbnails are made on request.

## `generating_audio`

| Item | When | Request |
|---|---|---|
| Narration | `allow_narration`, for every scene with a non-blank `narrator_text` whose narration is `none` or `failed` | one `narration` request per scene (4.3) |
| Music | `allow_music`, a non-blank `music_prompt`, `music_status` `none` or `failed` | one `music` request for the episode |

- The music request asks for at least the sum of the scenes' kept clip lengths, rounded up to the second.
- `allow_music` with a blank `music_prompt` → no request; the film has no music.
- How long a narration may be is a writing rule of the default `filmScript` prompt (2.2), not a check.
- The music file is saved at `storage/films/projects/{project-id}/episodes/{episode-id}/music.{ext}`.

## `assembling`

Runs in the batch worker with `ffmpeg` (4.2 § Stages); no provider is called. Every scene, in `position` order, must have
its clip `done`.

### Per scene

1. Kept part: `trim_start_ms` to `trim_end_ms` (or the clip's end).
2. Narration is used when `allow_narration` is on and the scene's narration is `done`. It starts with the
   scene; the scene then ends 500 ms after the narration ends, but never after the kept part ends — a longer
   narration is cut there.
3. Sound: with narration, the clip's sound at volume 0.3 under the narration at 1.5; without, the clip's
   sound as is.
4. Picture: scaled to fit and padded to 1920×1080 (`landscape`) or 1080×1920 (`portrait`), 24 fps,
   H.264 CRF 18; sound AAC 192 kb/s, 48 kHz, stereo.

### Whole film

5. Join the scenes in order.
6. Music, when `allow_music` and `music_status = done`: mixed under the whole film at `FILM_MUSIC_VOLUME`,
   cut at the film's end with a 2 s fade-out; a shorter track plays once.
7. Subtitles: one `.srt` cue per scene whose narration was used — from the scene's start in the film to
   start + narration length, capped at the scene's end; text = `narrator_text`. No cue → no `.srt`.
8. `burn_subtitles`: the cues are also burned into the picture in `subtitle_style`:

| `subtitle_style` | Look |
|---|---|
| `clean_white` | white text, thin dark outline, soft shadow |
| `cinematic_gold` | antique gold text, dark outline, wide margins |
| `cyber_amber` | glowing amber text, high-contrast outline |
| `emerald_hud` | light-green HUD text |

- The film is not cut to `target_duration_s`; the target only sets how many scenes the script has.
- Work files live in a temporary folder removed afterwards.
- Success: `video_path`, `subtitles_path` (or `null`), `duration_ms`, `needs_assembly = false`,
  `status = done`, `finished_at`.

| Failure | `error_message` |
|---|---|
| A scene has no clip | `Scene {n} has no clip` |
| `ffmpeg` error | `Assembly failed: {error}` |
| Longer than `FILM_ASSEMBLY_TIMEOUT_SEC` | `Assembly timed out after {N}s` |

### Files

| Path under `storage/films/projects/{project-id}/episodes/{episode-id}/` | Content |
|---|---|
| `source/` | the copied film analysis prompts and frames (4.2) |
| `scenes/{scene-id}/` | image, clip, narration (4.3) |
| `music.{ext}` | the music track |
| `output/{stem}.mp4` | the film |
| `output/{stem}.srt` | the subtitles |
| `output/thumbnail-candidate-{k}.{ext}` | thumbnail candidates 1–4, as the provider sent them |
| `output/{stem}-thumbnail.jpg` | the chosen thumbnail, 1280×720 |

`{stem}` = `{project name} - E{number, two digits} - {title}` made safe as the `{stem}` of 1.6; empty →
`episode`. Example: `Hong Nhat - E02 - Race Against Time`.

## Thumbnails

Not a stage: the user asks for them once the film looks right. Always 16:9 — the YouTube thumbnail size —
whatever the project orientation.

| Action | Rule | Result |
|---|---|---|
| Generate (`POST …/thumbnails`) | episode not `running` | 409 `Episode {number} is running — stop it first` |
| Generate | non-blank `thumbnail_prompt` | 409 `Episode {number} has no thumbnail prompt` |
| Generate | no `thumbnail` request of the episode `queued` or `processing` | 409 `Thumbnails are already being generated` |
| Choose (`POST …/thumbnail`) | `candidate` is 1–4 | 400 `Candidate must be 1-4` |
| Choose | that candidate's request is `done` | 409 `Thumbnail candidate {k} is not ready` |

- Generate queues four `thumbnail` requests, candidates 1–4, replacing the earlier candidates.
- Choose crops the candidate to fill 1280×720, saves `output/{stem}-thumbnail.jpg` and sets
  `thumbnail_path`.
- Thumbnails do not change `needs_assembly`.

## Download

`GET /films/episodes/{id}/download?file=video|subtitles|thumbnail` → attachment `{stem}.mp4`,
`{stem}.srt` or `{stem}-thumbnail.jpg`.

| Rule | Result |
|---|---|
| Episode exists | 404 `Episode {id} not found` |
| The file exists | 409 `Episode {number} has no {video \| subtitles \| thumbnail} yet` |

`GET /films/files/{path}` serves any file under `storage/films/` for previews; a missing file or a path
outside `storage/films/` → 404.
